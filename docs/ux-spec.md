## 5. UX quality bar

Every screen, once there are screens:

- Light and dark both use the tokens in `tokens/tokens.css`; no raw colours.
- Every status pairs colour with a word or an icon.
- Empty states name the next action; errors say what failed and how to recover.
- Keyboard focus is always visible.
