# The builder

The Nobody Builds mascot: a builder in a yellow hard hat and a dark suit, fast asleep face-down on a half-built
brick wall, three zzz floating up. It's the name, drawn. The same builder is the logo symbol.

| File | What it is | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| `builder.png` | The builder, cut out of its cream background (transparent) | Light surfaces: Paper, white, Hard Hat, Butter, Sky |
| `dark/builder.png` | The same art with a thin Paper sticker border | Night Shift, Blueprint and the dark theme only |
| `original/builder.png` | The GitHub avatar, byte for byte (460 × 460, on cream) | Editing and new exports |

All three are made by `scripts/build-assets.py` from `brand/source/`. Nothing was redrawn.

## Rules

1. Don't recolour, filter, redraw, stretch, skew or mirror the builder.
2. Don't add shadows, glows, gradients or outlines. The `dark/` sticker border is the one approved exception.
3. Don't give it new poses or wake it up with an edit. New poses need new artwork.
4. Don't show it taller than 280 px: that's the artwork's native size.
5. Keep a quarter of its height clear on every side.
6. Use `dark/builder.png` on dark surfaces; the outline disappears on Night Shift otherwise.

There's no animation yet, and the builder doesn't have a name yet. Until it does, write "the builder".
