## The brand at a glance

- **Name.** Write **Nobody Builds** in copy. The wordmark is lowercase **nobody builds**. Keep technical
  names (like the `Nobody-Builds` organisation) accurate.
- **Logo.** The builder is the symbol. Use the supplied masters. Never stretch, rotate, recolour, redraw or
  add effects. Keep a quarter of the symbol height clear on every side. [Logo rules →](/brand/logo/)
- **Mascot.** The same builder is the friendly face for empty states, onboarding and merch.
  [Meet the builder →](/brand/mascot/)
- **Colour.** Neutral first, accent second. Seven exact colours, and only tested text pairs. Blue does the
  work, yellow is the one bold moment. Never yellow text on Paper. Every status gets a word or an icon, not just a colour. [Colour →](/brand/colour/)
- **Type.** Gilroy for everything you read. Unbounded for one short, expressive phrase per layout.
  [Typography →](/brand/typography/)
- **Layout.** Use one bold moment, align to a simple grid, and let empty space do its job.
  [Graphics →](/brand/graphics/)
- **Voice.** Clear, warm and specific, with a dry sense of humour. Make the next step easy.
  [Voice →](/brand/voice/)

## Where it comes from

The visual identity comes from the builder illustration, the Nobody Builds avatar on GitHub. The palette is
built from its blue zzz and yellow hard hat, and every logo and mascot file is that same drawing, framed or bordered, never
redrawn. How screens behave will come from [Nobody Builds UX](/ux/introduction/), our interaction system,
once there's a product to design for.
