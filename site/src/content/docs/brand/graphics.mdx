## Built from bricks

Use brick shapes, running-bond rows and the zzz as quiet accents. Keep the builder itself intact.

1. **Use one bold moment.** One Blueprint panel, one Hard Hat highlight, one Unbounded phrase, or the
   builder. Not all at once.
2. **Align to a simple grid.** Bricks are a grid already.
3. **Let empty space do its job.**

## Shape

| Element | Rule |
|---|---|
| Controls (buttons, inputs) | 10 px radius |
| Cards and windows | 16 px radius |
| Tags and pills | Fully round |
| Shadows | None |
| Hairlines | Night Shift at 18% |

## Icons

Simple icons with the builder's thick, friendly outline.

- **24 px grid, 2 px stroke.**
- **Rounded ends, consistent weight.**
- **Always pair unfamiliar icons with labels.** Navigation is text-only.

## Imagery

**Show real builds:** hands, tools, half-finished projects and the mess that comes with them, in soft daylight.
Keep the work in focus.

Avoid staged stock photos, perfect desks and busy backdrops.
