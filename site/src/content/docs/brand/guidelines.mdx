The brand guidelines are the source of truth for the logo, colour, type, icons and voice. Everything else
on this site follows them.

## All 17 pages

Select a page to open it at full size (1440 × 1000).

<GuidelineGallery />

The pages are generated by `scripts/build-guidelines.py` from the logo masters, the builder, the fonts and the
palette in this repository, so they always match the kit. Rebuild them after any change with
`python scripts/build-assets.py && python scripts/build-guidelines.py`. The "To fill in" line on page 02 (what
Nobody Builds makes) is a placeholder: change it in the script and rebuild.
