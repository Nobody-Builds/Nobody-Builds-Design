## Sound like a builder who's in on the joke

Clear, warm and specific. Make the next step easy. The humour is in the name; the copy just has to be helpful.

| Say | Skip |
|---|---|
| <span class="nb-say">Nothing built yet. Lay the first brick.</span> | No records found in the database. |
| <span class="nb-say">Saved. Back to work.</span> | Your changes have been successfully persisted! |
| <span class="nb-say">Couldn't save. Try again.</span> | Oops! Somebody fell asleep on the job. |

## Rules of thumb

- **Say what happened, then what to do next.** An empty state names the next action. An error says what
  failed and how to recover.
- **Use plain words.** Projects, parts and builds, not assets and entities.
- **Joke when it's going well, never when it isn't.** A sleepy empty state is fine. An error is not the place
  for a punchline, and no exclamation marks for errors.

## Naming

- In copy, write **Nobody Builds**: two words, both capitalized.
- The wordmark is lowercase **nobody builds** and only ever appears as the logo artwork.
- Keep technical names accurate: the `Nobody-Builds` organisation, the `Nobody-Builds-Design` repository and
  `nobody-builds.github.io` stay as they are.
