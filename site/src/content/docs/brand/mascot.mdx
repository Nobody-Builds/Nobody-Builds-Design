## Meet the builder

The builder wears a yellow hard hat and a dark suit, and is fast asleep face-down on a half-built brick
wall, three zzz floating up. It's the name, drawn: nobody builds.

Every file is in [`mascot/`](https://github.com/Nobody-Builds/NobodyBuilds-Design/tree/main/mascot), cut from
the Nobody Builds avatar. Nothing was redrawn.

| Folder | What it is | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| `mascot/` | **The builder**, cut out of its cream background, transparent PNG | Light surfaces, docs, the website, stickers |
| `mascot/dark/` | **The builder for dark surfaces**, with a thin Paper sticker border | Night Shift and the dark theme's panels only |
| `mascot/original/` | **The original avatar**, byte for byte, on its original cream square | Editing and new exports: nothing is lost |

**On dark surfaces**, the builder's thick dark outline and suit almost disappear against Night Shift, so use
the file from `dark/`: its Paper die-cut border keeps the builder readable, and the art inside is untouched.
On Paper, white and the light brand colours, use the plain file.

## Why a builder asleep on the job

The name is a dare: nobody builds. The mascot answers it with a straight face.

| What the builder does | What it says about Nobody Builds |
|---|---|
| **Fell asleep on the job.** | We don't take ourselves too seriously. |
| **On a wall that's already half built.** | The work is real, and it's started. |
| **Still wearing the hard hat.** | Even resting, ready to build. |
| **Sleeps on the bricks, not in a bed.** | Close to the work. Hands-on, not hands-off. |

## Colour

The [brand colours](/brand/colour/) come from the builder: Blueprint from the zzz, Hard Hat from the
hat, Brick from the wall. Paper is a cooler, daylight take on the
drawing's cream background. The skin tone, the suit greys and the shading on the
bricks belong to the artwork; don't reuse them as UI colours.

## Where the builder shows up

The builder is the logo **and** the mascot, so the difference is in how it's used.

| As the mascot, on its own | As the logo, with the wordmark or the app tile |
|---|---|
| Empty states, onboarding, loading, success, 404 and error pages | The site header, favicon and app icon |
| The website hero, docs, social posts | Legal pages and formal documents |
| Stickers, merch and event signage | Anywhere the brand must be identified |

The builder counts as the layout's [one bold moment](/brand/graphics/).

## Keep the builder intact

1. **Don't recolour, filter or redraw** the builder. Use these files as they are.
2. **Don't stretch, skew or mirror** it.
3. **Don't add effects** such as shadows, glows, gradients or outlines. The one exception is the Paper
   sticker border of the approved `dark/` file; don't draw your own.
4. **Don't wake the builder up** with an edit, or give it a different pose. New poses need new artwork.
5. **Don't scale up the PNGs.** 280 px tall is the largest the builder gets.
6. **Keep clear space** of a quarter of the builder's height on every side.
7. **Use the `dark/` file on dark surfaces.**
