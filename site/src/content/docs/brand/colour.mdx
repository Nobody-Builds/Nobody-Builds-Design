## Seven colours. One clear system.

Exact sRGB values, built from the builder: the zzz blue and the hard hat yellow lead, the bricks add
warmth, and a cool blue-white Paper is the canvas. Three core colours, three accents and a daylight canvas.
These come straight from
[`tokens/tokens.json`](https://github.com/Nobody-Builds/NobodyBuilds-Design/blob/main/tokens/tokens.json).

## Neutral first, accent second

Most of any layout is Paper and Night Shift. Blueprint carries actions, Sky opens up whole sections, Hard
Hat is the one moment of energy, and Butter and Brick stay small.

- **Blueprint** is the primary brand colour and the action colour: the builder's zzz, taken deeper so
  Paper text passes on it.
- **Hard Hat** is energy and emphasis: the yellow of the builder's hat, one bold moment per layout.
- **Sky** can fill whole sections, like the home page hero, and carries supporting information.
- **Butter** is a small highlight. **Brick** is a warm accent and the warning colour. Both take Night Shift
  text.
- **Paper** is a cool blue-white, not cream, so the whole palette reads like daylight.
- **Night Shift** is a deep navy rather than black, so the dark side of the palette stays blue.

## Readable by design

Use only tested text pairings, and add a word or an icon to every status.

<Pairings />

- Normal text needs **≥ 4.5:1**; large text needs **≥ 3:1**. Large means 24 px regular or 18.7 px bold.
- **Avoid yellow or Brick text on Paper.** Hard Hat only reaches 1.49:1 and Brick 2.80:1. Use Blueprint
  for coloured text.
- Ratios follow WCAG 2.2 *Contrast (Minimum)* and *Use of Color*. `scripts/build-tokens.py` checks every
  pair and refuses to write the tokens if one fails.

The full light and dark palettes, with every ratio computed, are in the
[token reference](/tokens/reference/) and the [UI brand spec](/specs/ui-brand-spec/).
