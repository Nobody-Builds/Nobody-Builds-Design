## Visual identity

The builder illustration is the Nobody Builds avatar on GitHub; the untouched 460 × 460 file is kept in
`brand/source/avatar-460.png` and `mascot/original/`. The palette is built from its blue zzz and yellow
hard hat, and every logo, icon and mascot file is that drawing, cut out, framed or bordered by
`scripts/build-assets.py`.

The structure of this kit and site follows the
[Space Works design system](https://github.com/SpaceWorks-HQ/SpaceWorks-Design): the same folders, token
names and page layout, with the Nobody Builds identity in place of Space Works'.

The light/dark switch in the site header uses the same animation as
[Space Works Events](https://github.com/SpaceWorks-HQ/SpaceWorks-Events): the new theme grows out of the
toggle as a soft-edged circle, falls back to a colour cross-fade, and switches instantly with reduced motion.

## Fonts

- **Gilroy** Regular, Medium, Semibold and Bold are the same files as in the Space Works kit: format
  conversions of the owner's installed files, included at the owner's request. Gilroy is not an open-source
  font; use it under the applicable Gilroy licence.
- **Unbounded** is covered by the SIL Open Font License, in `fonts/LICENSES/OFL-Unbounded.txt`.
- The legacy Outfit face is covered by `fonts/LICENSES/OFL-Outfit.txt`.
